# Kodar Clips

Kodar Clips is a Windows desktop app for content creators. It turns long videos into short clips with AI, adds captions, credits and eye-catching covers, and helps you publish to your own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. A dashboard shows your profile, followers and the performance of your recent posts.
